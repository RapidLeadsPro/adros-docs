# Switching between your AI and the App

> **Draft — planned features, not yet available.** This guide describes the planned
> shared workspace experience. The activity tools, file versions, trash, and
> cross-channel summaries below must not be treated as available in your account.
> This page is being prepared for review and is held from publication until the
> required capabilities are enabled for the first owners.

## One workspace across both channels

The planned experience will let you continue work in the App or through your own
connected AI without creating a separate history. Both channels will read the
same saved activity and file records for the selected brand and offer.

The App will show files, decisions, and work produced by Adros agents. Your own AI
will be able to read saved workspace records and write back its work through MCP.
A file produced by your AI will remain attributed to your AI when you open it in
the App. Opening, moving, or approving a file will not change who produced it.

Before continuing, you will select the same brand and offer in either channel.
Research and shared brand facts will sit under **Whole brand**. Within each brand
and offer, files and activity will use the same stage names:

- Step 1 Research
- Step 2A Big Idea
- Step 2B Structure and Forecast
- Step 3 Creative
- Step 4 Deploy
- Step 5 Optimization

## Who produced the work

The planned attribution will distinguish the producer from the channel used to
access the workspace. Using the App will not, by itself, mean that Adros produced
a file.

| Producer label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Your name | Work recorded as produced by you, the owner. |
| Adros followed by the team name | Work produced by the named Adros agent or team. |
| Your AI via MCP, followed by the connected client name | Work produced by your own connected AI. The client name will identify the connection, not the brand. |
| Adros system | A recorded system action or handoff. |

Activity will separately identify whether it came through the App, external MCP,
or a system handoff. Missing attribution will be shown as unavailable; the system
will not guess the producer from a model, file location, or access channel.

**Produced**, **Updated**, and **Approved** will have separate timestamps where
recorded. They will use your local time, with a relative hint. An update will not
be presented as an approval, and a missing time will not be invented.

## How files and history will work

Files will organize artifacts by brand, offer, and stage. Command Center will
hold the activity ledger and decisions, and the Workspace stage drawer will show
the same artifact records.

Every save will create a new revision. **Current** will mark the selected revision;
it will not replace or erase older revisions. A version list will let you inspect
the history, with comparisons for supported text and workbook artifacts.

The planned file states will be:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Work still in preparation. |
| Current | The revision marked as the current version. |
| Approved | A recorded approval exists for the revision. |
| Locked | The revision is locked under its workflow. |
| Stale | The revision needs review because its context has changed. |
| Superseded | A later revision has taken its place. |
| Unreceipted | An existing file has no matching artifact receipt; its missing history will not be fabricated. |

Moving or deleting a file will leave an activity record and a receipt explaining
what happened. Deletion will move the file to trash for 30 days, during which it
can be restored. After that retention period, deletion will be permanent and will
also have a receipt. These are planned protections; do not rely on them for files
deleted before this capability becomes available.

## Picking up where the other channel stopped

A planned **Since you were here** card will summarize the other channel's latest
actions for a brand or offer. Your connected AI's resume information will include
recent activity and the current files for each offer, so it can inspect saved work
before continuing.

These summaries will describe recorded actions. They will not schedule work,
approve a decision, or start a new run on their own.

## Planned tools for your connected AI

The planned MCP read tools are:

| Tool | Intended result |
| --- | --- |
| `get_adros_workspace_activity` | Saved activity for the selected brand, with offer, stage, producer, and channel filters. |
| `list_adros_workspace_files` | Saved file records and their provenance for the same workspace. |
| `read_adros_workspace_artifact` | A specifically identified artifact revision, with its receipt and provenance. |

Activity and file lists will support paging and will use the same underlying
records as the App. Artifact reads will require the matching brand, task, stage,
revision, and receipt references. A missing or unavailable record will be reported
as unavailable rather than replaced with guessed content.

These tools are planned as free reads of saved workspace records. They will not
start paid research or creative generation. Separately chargeable work will
remain subject to its credit quote and approval; switching channels will not
itself authorize that work.

Until these capabilities are enabled, this draft is a description of intended
behavior, not an instruction to call tools that may be unavailable.
