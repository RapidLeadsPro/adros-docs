# Adros Help Center

Welcome! These guides are written for **advertisers and business owners** using the Adros dashboard at [app.adros.ai](https://app.adros.ai). No technical background needed.

If you're a developer integrating with Adros over MCP or REST, head to the [developer Quickstart](/quickstart) and [Integrations](/integrations/overview) instead.

## The one rule to remember

**Nothing goes live without your explicit approval.** Adros researches, plans, and prepares — but every campaign plan and every creative passes through you before anything is deployed to your ad accounts.
