# Connect Shopify and Cometly for sales attribution

Ad platforms tell you about clicks. Your store and your attribution tool tell you about **sales**. Connecting them gives Adros the full picture — so plans are built on real customer data and optimization decisions follow real revenue, not just platform metrics.

Both connections are optional, but strongly recommended.

## Connect Shopify

Connecting Shopify lets Adros sync your **customers and orders**. That real data — order values, product mix, customer locations, repeat-purchase behavior — feeds directly into your personas and KPI forecast, making your campaign plan much more grounded than industry assumptions.

1. In the dashboard sidebar, open **Connections**.
2. On the **Shopify** card, click **Connect** and follow the prompts to authorize access to your store.
3. Once connected, Adros syncs customers and orders for the brand the store is assigned to.

A few notes:

- Connecting is for **data sync** — it's how Adros reads your store's customers and orders for planning and measurement.
- You can disconnect at any time from the same Connections page.
- **Not on Shopify?** WooCommerce stores can connect from the same page, and any store can upload a CSV/XLSX export of customers or orders instead (during onboarding or via **Files**).

## Connect Cometly

[Cometly](https://www.cometly.com) is an ad attribution platform: it tracks which ads, campaigns, and channels actually produce your sales. If you use Cometly, connecting it lets Adros read that attribution data when evaluating performance — so "what's working" is judged by revenue, not clicks alone.

1. In the dashboard sidebar, open **Integrations** (one card per brand-level integration).
2. On the **Cometly** card, enter:
   - **Cometly API key** — each brand has its own Cometly Space with its own key. Find it in your Cometly dashboard under **API → Generate Key**.
   - **Space ID** — the numeric ID of your Cometly Space (usually the prefix of the API key).
   - **Space name** — a label so you can recognize this Space later.
3. Save. The integration is scoped to the brand you configured it for — if you manage multiple brands, each connects its own Cometly Space.

## How Adros uses this data

- **Better plans:** personas and KPI forecasts calibrated with your real order values and customer mix.
- **Honest measurement:** forecast-vs-live tracking in **KPI Performance** grounded in actual sales.
- **Smarter optimization:** Step 5 recommendations weigh real revenue attribution, not just platform-reported metrics.

Your data stays scoped to your workspace and is used only to plan and measure your own campaigns.
