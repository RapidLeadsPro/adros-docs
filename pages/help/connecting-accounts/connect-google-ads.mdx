# Connect your Google Ads account

Connecting Google Ads lets Adros read your account's performance and — only after your explicit approval — deploy campaigns to it.

## Before you start

- You need a **Google Ads account** and a Google login with access to it.
- Pop-ups must be allowed for `app.adros.ai` — the connection opens in a secure Google sign-in popup.

## Connect in three steps

1. In the dashboard sidebar, open **Connections** (or use the **Connect Google Ads** button during onboarding / on the Get Started checklist).
2. On the **Google Ads** card, click **Connect**. A secure Google sign-in popup opens — choose the Google account that has access to your Google Ads account and grant the requested access.
3. Back in Adros, the Google Ads card shows **Connected**. Then assign the connected account to the right brand on the same page.

If you sign in with a login that can access multiple Google Ads accounts (for example through a manager account), make sure the right account ends up assigned to the right brand under **Brands**.

## What access you're granting

- **Reading:** campaigns, keywords, and performance metrics — used for research, planning, and reporting.
- **Managing ads on your behalf:** Adros stages campaign changes and applies them **only when you approve a deployment**. Connecting by itself changes nothing in your account.
- **Billing stays with Google.** Your ad spend is billed by Google to the payment method in your Google Ads account, exactly as before. Adros never charges your ad account.

## Reconnecting and disconnecting

- **Reconnect:** if access expires or you change your Google password, the Connections page flags it — click **Reconnect** and sign in again.
- **Disconnect:** click **Disconnect** on the Google Ads card and confirm. Adros immediately loses access. Campaigns already live in your account keep running — they belong to you, not to Adros.

## Troubleshooting

- **The popup doesn't open** — allow pop-ups for `app.adros.ai` and retry.
- **"Please log in first"** — your dashboard session expired; sign in to Adros again, then retry.
- **Connected the wrong Google account** — disconnect, then reconnect and pick the correct Google login in the popup.
- **Your account isn't listed** — verify your Google login actually has access to the Google Ads account (check Access & security inside Google Ads).
