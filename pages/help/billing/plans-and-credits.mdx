# Plans and credits: what's included and what actions cost

Adros pricing is simple: a monthly plan gives you a **credit allowance**, and metered actions spend credits. Plenty of everyday actions are free.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits per month |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $97/month | 200 |
| **Pro** | $297/month | 500 |

New accounts start with a **7-day free trial of the Pro plan** — full Pro features and the complete workflow, at no charge. See [Managing your subscription](/help/billing/managing-your-subscription) for how the trial works and what happens when it ends.

Every workspace includes **3 team seats** at no extra charge. You can add or remove extra seats yourself in-app at **$5 per seat / month** — it's fully self-serve. Team members share the workspace owner's credit wallet.

## What actions cost

Credits meter the heavy lifting — research runs, plan building, creative production, and platform actions.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| **Step 1** — managed research session | 12 |
| Citation-backed web research pass | 4 |
| Landing page audit | 1 |
| Brand profile scan | 1 |
| Keyword research | 5–15, depending on depth |
| **Step 2** — full Structure & Forecast run | 20 |
| Focused plan repair | 6 |
| **Step 3** — creative package (copy + creative) | 8 |
| Image generation (per image, by quality tier) | 1–5 |
| **Step 4** — successful platform action on Meta/Google | 1 each |
| Deploy review / issue-solving session | 6 |

Three fairness rules built into metering:

- **Failed platform actions cost nothing.** Only successful actions are charged.
- **No double-charging.** Each managed task is charged once — resuming or retrying the same task doesn't charge again.
- **Estimates before big spends.** Larger image batches show you the credit estimate before generating.

## Always free

These never use credits, on any plan:

- **CEO chat** — ask questions, give direction, get status anytime.
- **Saving your business context and personas** — keeping Adros's memory of your business up to date.
- **Pattern library lookups** — browsing the performance-tested ad pattern database.
- **Report exports** — downloading your reports.
- **Connecting and managing accounts** — Connections and Integrations don't consume credits.

And to be completely clear: **your ad spend is never paid in credits.** Media budgets are billed by Meta and Google directly to your ad accounts — credits only pay for the work Adros does.

## How your credit wallet works

- **Monthly credits first.** Your plan allowance is spent before any purchased top-up credits.
- **Monthly credits reset** with each billing cycle — unused plan credits don't roll over.
- **Top-up credits never expire.** Need more in a busy month? Top-up packs are available from the **Plan & Credits** page: **100 credits for $10**, **300 credits for $30**, or **600 credits for $60**.
- **Running low?** Adros warns you before you hit zero.
- **At zero**, metered actions pause safely: a task that needs credits shows a clear "Add credits" blocker in Command Center and waits — nothing breaks, and free actions (like CEO chat) keep working. Top up or wait for your monthly reset, then retry.

## Where to see your balance

Open **Plan & Credits** in the dashboard sidebar for your current plan, live balance, and a full usage history of what was charged and why.

For trial details, upgrades, and cancellation, see [Managing your subscription](/help/billing/managing-your-subscription).
