# Managing your subscription, trial, and cancellations

Everything about your plan lives on the **Plan & Credits** page in the dashboard sidebar: your current plan, credit balance, usage history, and plan actions.

## The free trial

- **A 7-day free trial of the Pro plan, $0.** You get full Pro features — enough to complete onboarding, connect your accounts, run research, and review your first campaign plan.
- The trial is the full product — same agents, same workflow, same approval gates.
- **When the trial ends, your subscription continues as Pro ($297/month, 500 credits) automatically, unless you cancel before the trial ends.** There's no surprise: cancel any time during the 7 days and you won't be charged. Your connected accounts, research, and plans are kept either way, so you pick up right where you left off.

## Upgrading or changing plans

- Move between **Starter** ($97/month, 200 credits) and **Pro** ($297/month, 500 credits) from the **Plan & Credits** page.
- **Upgrading mid-cycle takes effect immediately and is prorated.** You're charged the prorated difference now for the rest of the current cycle, and the new rate applies from your next invoice (standard Stripe proration). Your monthly credit allowance follows your plan and refreshes each billing cycle.
- If you regularly run out mid-month, [top-up credits](/help/billing/plans-and-credits#how-your-credit-wallet-works) are often simpler than upgrading — they never expire.

## Cancelling

- **Cancel anytime** from the **Plan & Credits** page.
- **You keep access until the end of your current billing period.** Cancelling stops future billing — it doesn't cut you off immediately, so you can keep working through the time you've already paid for.
- **Credits already used are not refunded.** Cancelling ends future charges; it doesn't refund credits you've already spent.
- **Your ad accounts are unaffected.** Adros never owns your campaigns — anything live in your Meta or Google Ads accounts stays live and fully under your control. You can also [disconnect your accounts](/help/connecting-accounts/connect-meta) at any time, before or after cancelling.

## Refunds

Credits already used are not refunded. If something went wrong with a charge, contact support and we'll look at it case by case.

## Failed runs and credit concerns

- **Failed platform actions are never charged**, and managed tasks are charged only once even across retries.
- If you believe a run charged credits without delivering, open the task in **Command Center**, check the timeline, and raise it in the task thread — the full usage history on **Plan & Credits** shows exactly what was charged and why.

## Getting help

Ask the **CEO** in the app for anything plan-related — it's free and can point you to the right page. For billing issues the CEO can't resolve, contact the Adros team through the dashboard.
