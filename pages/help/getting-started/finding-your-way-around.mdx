# Finding your way around

The Adros dashboard at [app.adros.ai](https://app.adros.ai) has four surfaces you'll use most days. Here's what each one is for.

## Command Center — your daily workroom

**Command Center** is your cockpit: what needs review, what's blocked, what's running, and what's waiting for your approval.

Each piece of work is a **task** with its own discussion thread — and that thread is the single source of truth for the work:

- **Read the timeline** to see exactly what each agent did and why.
- **Approve or request changes** directly on the task. Your change-request feedback stays attached to the task, so nothing gets lost.
- **Mention an agent** to get answers in the same thread: `@Adros` asks the CEO to reply, while `@CMO`, `@CD`, `@Deployer`, `@Research`, and `@Reports` ask that specialist a consult-only question — they answer without starting new work.
- **Nothing starts on its own.** Specialist work begins only after your explicit approval or routing confirmation.

If a task shows a blocker (for example, "Add credits before this run can start"), the blocker card tells you exactly what's needed to continue.

## Ad Structure — your campaign plan, mapped

**Ad Structure** shows your entire campaign plan and how it compares to what's live in your ad accounts:

- The full tree: campaigns → ad sets → ads, with budgets, audiences, and destinations.
- **Plan vs Live** — what's planned, what's already running, and badges for what will be **new**, **changed**, or **paused** when you approve.
- Drill-down drawers per entity, including real keyword phrases for search campaigns.
- Attached copy and creatives for each ad, plus landing-page audit context.
- A history of changes, so you can always see what was done and when.

This is the page to study before approving a Step 2 plan or a deployment.

## KPI Performance — forecast vs reality

**KPI Performance** tracks the numbers: the KPI forecast your plan committed to, next to live results once campaigns are running. Each KPI row links to the same planned or live entities you see in Ad Structure, so you can jump from "this number looks off" straight to the campaign behind it.

## Adros CEO — your single point of contact

The **Adros CEO** page is a chat workspace. Talk to it like you'd talk to an account manager:

- Describe goals in plain language; the CEO turns them into clearly scoped work for the specialists.
- Before a specialist runs, the CEO confirms the scope with you — so you always know what was asked for.
- For questions about a specific task, reply in that task's thread in Command Center instead; the conversation stays attached to the work.

CEO chat is free — it never uses credits.

## The rest of the sidebar, at a glance

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Get Started** | Your setup checklist — connect accounts, complete onboarding steps. |
| **Analytics** | Campaign performance from your connected ad accounts. |
| **Files** | Everything uploaded or produced — research reports, customer data, creatives. |
| **Creatives** | Browse all generated ad creatives in one place. |
| **Plan & Credits** | Your subscription, credit balance, and usage. See [Billing & Credits](/help/billing/plans-and-credits). |
| **Workflow** | The 5-step pipeline view with each stage's deliverables. |
| **Brands** | Manage the brands (businesses) Adros works on, and which ad accounts belong to each. |
| **Connections** | Connect or disconnect Meta, Google Ads, and your store. See [Connecting Your Accounts](/help/connecting-accounts/connect-meta). |
| **Integrations** | Per-brand integrations like Cometly for sales attribution. |
| **Settings** | Your profile and workspace settings. |
