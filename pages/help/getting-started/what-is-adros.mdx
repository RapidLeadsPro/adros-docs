# What is Adros? Your AI ads team, explained

Adros is an **AI ads team** that takes your business and your offer and turns them into live, optimized ad campaigns on **Meta** and **Google Ads** — with you approving every important decision along the way.

Instead of one generic chatbot, Adros works like a small agency staffed by specialists.

## Meet the team

| Agent | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| **CEO** | Your single point of contact. You talk to the CEO in plain language; it captures what you want, routes the work to the right specialist, presents results back to you, and holds the approval gates. |
| **Research** | Studies your market: competitors, customer language, keywords, and your landing pages. |
| **CMO** | Builds your campaign plan — campaign structure, budgets, audiences, and a KPI forecast — and later proposes ongoing optimizations. |
| **Creative Director** | Writes your final ad copy and produces ad creatives, working inside the plan you approved. |
| **Deployer** | Pushes your approved campaigns to Meta and Google Ads, and keeps an audit trail of every change. |

Behind the team sits a pattern library of **4,000+ performance-tested ad patterns**, so plans start from what has actually worked — not from a blank page.

## How the work flows

Adros runs a 5-step pipeline:

1. **Research** — learn your market, competitors, and customers.
2. **Structure & Forecast** — design the campaign plan and forecast KPIs. **You approve it.**
3. **Creative** — produce final copy and creatives. **You approve them.**
4. **Deploy** — push the approved campaigns to your connected ad accounts.
5. **Optimize** — propose adjustments based on live performance. **You approve changes.**

Each step is covered in detail in [The 5-Step Workflow](/help/workflow/step-1-research).

## What Adros does

- Researches your market, competitors, and customer voice before spending anything on media.
- Designs complete campaign structures for Meta and Google Ads with budgets, audiences, and KPI forecasts.
- Writes ad copy and generates ad creatives for your review.
- Deploys approved campaigns to your connected ad accounts and records every change.
- Monitors performance and proposes optimizations — budget shifts, audience tweaks, pausing underperformers.

## What Adros doesn't do

- **It never goes live without your approval.** No campaign, ad, or budget change reaches your ad account until you explicitly approve it.
- **It doesn't spend your ad budget itself.** Media spend stays between you and Meta/Google — Adros manages campaigns inside accounts you own and control.
- **It doesn't replace your judgment.** Adros prepares plans and recommendations; you stay the decision-maker at every gate.
- **It doesn't lock you in.** You can disconnect your ad accounts at any time from the Connections page.
