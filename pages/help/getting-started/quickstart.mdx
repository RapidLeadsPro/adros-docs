# Quickstart: from signup to your first campaign plan

This guide takes you from a fresh signup to your first campaign plan waiting for your approval.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up at [app.adros.ai](https://app.adros.ai). New accounts start on a **7-day free trial of the Pro plan** — full Pro features, enough to run research and see your first plan before deciding on a paid plan. (See [Plans and credits](/help/billing/plans-and-credits) for what credits are.)

## 2. Complete onboarding

Onboarding is a short guided interview about your business. The better you answer, the better your plan — this is where Adros learns:

- **Your business and offer** — what you sell, to whom, and what makes it different.
- **Your market** — where your customers are and what languages they speak.
- **Your budget and channels** — how much you plan to spend and whether you want Meta, Google Ads, or both.
- **Your brand rules** — anything Adros must always (or never) say in your ads.

You can also **connect your ad accounts during onboarding** — a secure popup opens, and you can close it and keep going even if you don't finish connecting right away.

## 3. Connect your ad accounts

If you skipped connecting during onboarding, go to **Connections** in the sidebar:

- [Connect your Meta ad account](/help/connecting-accounts/connect-meta)
- [Connect your Google Ads account](/help/connecting-accounts/connect-google-ads)

You need at least one connected ad account before campaigns can deploy. Connecting is read-safe: nothing changes in your ad account until you approve a deployment.

## 4. Share your customer data (recommended)

During onboarding — or anytime later — you can upload a CSV/XLSX of your last 12 months of customers or orders, or [connect Shopify](/help/connecting-accounts/connect-shopify-cometly) so Adros syncs it automatically.

Why it matters: real order values, product mix, locations, and repeat-customer signals make your personas and KPI forecast far more accurate. You can skip it, but adding it later may mean rerunning research, which uses additional credits.

## 5. Tell the CEO what you want

Open **Adros CEO** from the sidebar and describe your goal in plain language — for example:

> "I want to launch lead generation campaigns for my dental clinic in Singapore with a $2,000/month budget."

The CEO turns your request into a clearly scoped task and routes it to the right specialist. Your first stop is **Step 1 — Research**: Adros studies your market, competitors, and customer language. See [what Adros learns about your market](/help/workflow/step-1-research).

## 6. Review your first campaign plan

When Research is done, the CMO builds your **campaign plan and KPI forecast** (Step 2). You'll see it appear in:

- **Command Center** — the task asking for your review and approval.
- **Ad Structure** — the full plan: campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets, audiences.
- **KPI Performance** — what results the plan forecasts.

Read it, ask questions in the task thread, request changes — and when it looks right, **approve it**. That approval is what unlocks creative production. Nothing has touched your ad account yet, and nothing will until you approve deployment.

## What happens next

- **Step 3 — Creative:** review and approve ad copy and creatives. [Guide →](/help/workflow/step-3-creative)
- **Steps 4 & 5 — Deploy & Optimize:** approve go-live, then approve ongoing tuning. [Guide →](/help/workflow/steps-4-5-deploy-optimize)

Stuck at any point? Check [Finding Your Way Around](/help/getting-started/finding-your-way-around) or ask the CEO directly in the app — CEO chat is free and doesn't use credits.
