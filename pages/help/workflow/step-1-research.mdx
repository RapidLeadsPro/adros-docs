# Step 1 — Research: what Adros learns about your market

Good campaigns start with knowing the market. Before Adros plans anything, the **Research agent** builds a fact base about your business — so the plan in Step 2 rests on evidence, not guesses.

## What Research looks at

- **Your market** — demand, seasonality, and the competitive landscape for what you sell, where you sell it.
- **Your competitors** — who they are, what they promise, and how their landing pages are built.
- **Voice of customer** — the actual words customers use in reviews and forums to describe problems your product solves. This language later shapes your ad copy.
- **Keywords** — what your customers search for, with real search volume and cost data (mainly for Google Ads planning).
- **Your landing pages** — an audit of the pages your ads will send traffic to, flagging anything that could hurt conversion.
- **Your own customer data** — if you've connected Shopify or uploaded order history, Research uses real order values, locations, and repeat-purchase patterns to build personas.

## What you get

A research deliverable that becomes the foundation for your campaign plan:

- Market and competitor findings with sources.
- Customer personas grounded in your data and customer language.
- Keyword findings with volumes and cost estimates.
- Landing page findings and fix recommendations.

You'll find it in the **Command Center** task (with the full timeline of what the agent did) and in **Files**.

## How it starts

Tell the CEO what you want to achieve (see [Quickstart](/help/getting-started/quickstart)). The CEO scopes it with you and routes a research task. You can also ask for focused research later — for example, "research competitors for my new product line."

## You stay in control

Research doesn't touch your ad accounts — it only reads and studies. When it's done, review the findings, ask questions in the task thread (`@Research` for the specialist, `@Adros` for the CEO), and request another pass if something important is missing. When you're satisfied, the findings flow into [Step 2 — Structure & Forecast](/help/workflow/step-2-structure-forecast).
